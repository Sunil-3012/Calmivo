The Story

Why I built Calmivo

Late one night during my final year of college, I found myself spiraling with anxiety but too exhausted to call anyone. I just needed something to talk to. Something patient. Something that wouldn't judge me for feeling overwhelmed at 2am.

I'm Sunil, a software engineer who started building Calmivo not because I saw a market opportunity, but because I genuinely needed something like it. The companion I wish I'd had.

Calmivo isn't trying to replace therapy. It's the bridge between feeling overwhelmed and feeling ready to take the next step.
